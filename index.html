Dear Ore,

A Birthday Letter · from Sochi, with love

Happy Birthday, Love. From the very first time I got to know you, there was something different about you — something radiant, something profound. You carry a light that brightens every space you enter, a grace that speaks volumes without words. And I celebrate you today for your kindness, your smarts, your unwavering spirit, and the quiet courage that defines everything you do.

As you step into this new chapter of life, my prayer is simple: that you walk in unshakable joy, in undeniable favour, and in the fullness of all that God has in store for you. Let His wisdom be your guide, His peace your anchor, and His love your ever-present comfort. Every door meant for you opens effortlessly at your declaration, and every season ahead unfolds with purpose and beauty beyond what you can imagine right now.

You are destined for impact, and I have no doubt that the world is only beginning to witness the incredible, radiant woman you are becoming. Keep shining, keep thriving, and never stop believing in the wonder that is you. The world shifts slightly on its axis when you walk into a room — that is not nothing. That is everything.

And when the days feel heavy, when doubts creep in, when life feels overwhelming — pause. Breathe. You are not alone. You are deeply loved, divinely guided, and endlessly capable. Strength is within you, peace is around you, and grace is upon you. Nothing is too hard, nothing is impossible, and no moment of uncertainty can change the greatness that is already inside you.

You were created to impact creation, and nothing will or can stop you from becoming all that God has called you to be. Whenever you need a reminder, hold on to these words:

Isaiah 60:1-2 "Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee."
Romans 8:19 "For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the daughters of God."

Even in the harshest of times, you are still ordained to shine, still empowered to manifest. This is not wishful thinking — it is your inheritance. I hope these words find you wherever you are and remind you of exactly who you are.

With all the love I have, Sochi

Three Poems for You

Written in reflection of who you are

I

Genuinity Speaks

As bright as the morning sunrise is …
the sight, the sheer beauty —
I've come to find something that shines brighter,
warmer than the warmest golden hour …
a smile so genuine it speaks,
one I behold … and somehow
my own cheeks rise in reflection,
as if intimacy finds a platform of reflection.

A wonder that never grows familiar,
yet somehow never feels unfamiliar —
always new, always known,
like a memory my heart remembers
but my eyes keep seeing for the very first time.

In that smile, I discovered a peace
I didn't know I was missing,
a quiet kind of magic that whispers to the soul,
reminding me that even in a world
deafened by chaos and clamor,
there are rare moments that feel as if
time itself has bowed its head,
allowing me to witness the radiance
of one soul in full, unshakable clarity.

And maybe that is the miracle of you:
that in a universe of fleeting lights,
blinding and momentary,
you remain constant, unwavering —
a beacon that doesn't simply shine,
but illuminates the quiet corners of the heart.

II

Face to Face with Light

I run, I run — where can I go?
I look, I look — what can I see?
A dark room where darkness is most dark,
a place where hope lingers only as a ghost.
The shadows whisper the history of the room,
of the love that once filled its walls —
a love my very soul bears witness to as true.
My face stands frozen — in awe, yet doomed —
for I was sentenced, forever,
to the quiet death that shadows bring.

Yet in the stillness, a light breaks through,
a warmth spreads, breathing life into the shadows,
and Love Himself steps back into the room.
Not the love that fades,
but the Love that died and rose again,
the Love that carries scars
as proof that He searched every grave for me.

My life was doomed, so He gave me His.
My heart was weary, so He gave me a new one.
My soul, once in despair,
comes face to face with its first love.
For this is salvation:
God stepping into my darkness,
pouring His wounds into my hopelessness,
calling my name with a tenderness
that death itself could not silence.

This is the miracle of His death:
that the Light entered the darkest room,
and instead of condemning me,
He stretched His wounded hands toward mine
and whispered,
"Live."

III

The Little Girl & the Tower

Chased by the wind of life, the dust rising,
the sky heavy, gloomy, and thick with shadows,
leaves swirling in obedience to the storm's rhythm.
A climax is reached as a little girl
and the "swirler" collide —
a collision of worlds, where fear rises
like smoke curling in the hollow of her chest.

Her little legs run, small head turning left and right,
each breath whispering "hopeless,"
each step bringing no progress,
until, far ahead, a Tower rises in the horizon,
its height stretching far beyond the stormy clouds.

She reaches for the old wooden door,
rickety and swaying, yet open, and steps inside.
There stands a Shepherd with no sheep,
and a staircase stretching endlessly upward.
Dusty, trembling, she hesitates —
until she sees in the Shepherd's gaze
a reflection deeper than words,
a smile that speaks millions without sound,
acknowledging every trauma, every fear.

Together they ascend,
His hands steady around hers,
each step revealing windows into the fading storm.
And with every glimpse downward, she sees
Purpose emerging from the chaos.

For as she climbs, the Abba Father walks beside her,
turning every fear into courage, every tear into testimony —
until she realizes:
the storm was never her enemy.
It was the pathway leading her home.

✦ A Word for You ✦
O

Owned

Even when the storms rise and the nights feel endless, you are fully known and cherished. Not by chance, not by circumstance, but by a love that claimed you first — carrying you, covering you, keeping you safe in every season. Every wave, every trial, every shadow that tries to shake you only reveals the care of the hands that never let go.

R

Remembered

You are known completely. Nothing behind you, nothing before you, and not even the doubts in your own heart can separate you from the One who calls you His. Every fear, every silence, every heavy thought is met with the whisper of His faithfulness: "I am with you." You are remembered, cherished, never forgotten — a treasure held in divine sight.

E

Empowered

Step forward, Ore, with quiet confidence. Every season you've walked through was not meant to consume you, but to reveal you. The love that carries you also strengthens and equips you. You are becoming exactly who you were always seen to be: radiant, resilient, and ready to shine into every room, every life, every quiet corner. Your light is unstoppable, and your story is just beginning to unfold.
